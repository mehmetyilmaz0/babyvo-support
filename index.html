Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a co-parent?
Go to Baby Profile → Invite → enter their email address.

How do I delete an entry?
Swipe left on any entry in the timeline to delete it.

I forgot my password. What should I do?
BabyVo uses email verification — no password needed. Just enter your email to receive a new login code.

Can I track multiple babies?
Yes. You can add multiple baby profiles from your account.
