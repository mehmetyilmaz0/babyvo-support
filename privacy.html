Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2025  ·  Effective: May 18, 2025

BabyVo ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of parents and caregivers who use our app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (used for login via one-time password)
  • Name (optional, used for display purposes)
  • Authentication tokens from Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In if you choose those methods

Baby Tracking Data

  • Baby profile: name, date of birth, sex
  • Feeding logs: type, duration, amount, timestamp, notes
  • Sleep logs: start/end time, location, notes
  • Diaper logs: type, timestamp, notes
  • Growth measurements: weight, height, head circumference
  • Doctor visit records and vaccine records

Usage Data

  • App interactions and feature usage (for improving the app)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Crash reports and error logs (anonymized)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the BabyVo service
  • To sync your data across devices and with co-parents you invite
  • To generate AI-powered insights about your baby's routines (processed via third-party AI service)
  • To send you notifications you have enabled
  • To process subscription payments
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

BabyVo uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication provider (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Apple Sign-In — optional authentication provider (Apple Privacy Policy)
  • Groq — AI inference service used to analyze baby activity patterns (Groq Privacy Policy). Only anonymized activity summaries are sent; no names or identifiers are included.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (RevenueCat Privacy Policy)

4. Data Sharing

Your data is shared only in the following circumstances:

  • Co-parents / caregivers: Users you explicitly invite can view and edit the shared baby profile.
  • Service providers: Only as necessary to operate the app (see Section 3).
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using platform-provided secure storage.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

BabyVo is designed for use by parents and caregivers (adults). The app tracks data about infants and children but is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access and download your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
